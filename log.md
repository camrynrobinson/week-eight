**definitions**

isovist
: what can be seen from any particular point

axial space
: straight-line visibility

integration
: (`shortest path`) number of steps from the street to all other street segments or rooms in a building (a little bit like closeness in a social network)

choice
: sort of like measuring flow betweenness. 

depth
: pick a point and measure steps to all other points. repeat for every point. areas with lowest values are 'deeper' in the graph.

**machine translation**
- Rosetta stone approach: take parallel texts and try to classify words/sentences/ideas in one language by reference to another (like matching pictures of cats with the textual 'cat' label) (essentially teaching the network grammar rules)
- Madlibs approach: mask out key words and then predict the missing word based on the words around it.

attention mechanism
: determines importance of each component in a sequence relative to the other components of the sequence. 

- if you put the mask at the end of the Madlibs sentence and don't give it anything specific to translate from, it will just make up whatever it thinks is the next best answer. thus genAI was born.
